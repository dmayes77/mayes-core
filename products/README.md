# Products

Products are sellable configurations of Core. Each adds only what its business needs.

| Product | Repo today | Moves here when |
|---|---|---|
| ShopFlow (Shopify storefronts) | `dmayes77/shopflow`, `dmayes77/shopflow-code` | re-based on Core v2 after the $500 site launches |
| DetailFlow (auto detailers) | `dmayes77/detailflow` | re-based on Core v2 after the $500 site launches |

When a product moves in, it gets `products/<name>/` with its own `javascript/`, `dist/`, `docs/`, and only product code: everything generic is promoted into Core first.
