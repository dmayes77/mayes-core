# Search Strategy

Read for business discovery, search intent, research, site structure, platform changes, and ethical search decisions. Verify time-sensitive platform guidance against current primary sources.

## Mayes Digital Business Philosophy

Mayes Digital serves businesses that need a professional online presence without becoming dependent on paid advertising.

The objective is to establish a digital foundation that communicates the quality, legitimacy, and value of the business.

### Strategic priorities

- Establish an authoritative business website.
- Improve organic discoverability.
- Communicate services clearly.
- Demonstrate authentic credibility.
- Support local customer discovery.
- Generate relevant customer inquiries.
- Build sustainable digital assets.
- Measure meaningful business outcomes.

### Business positioning

Mayes Digital operates according to the following belief:

**Your work already speaks for itself. Your online presence should too.**

Organic visibility should amplify the quality and credibility of an existing business.

It must not manufacture credibility that the business has not earned.

## Organic Visibility Framework

Evaluate organic visibility through six interconnected areas.

### Foundation

The business has an accessible, technically reliable, and professionally presented website.

### Discoverability

Search engines and relevant platforms can discover and understand the business.

### Relevance

Website content accurately addresses customer search intent.

### Credibility

The business demonstrates authentic experience, legitimacy, and value.

### Conversion

Customers can take meaningful actions after discovering the business.

### Measurement

The business can evaluate visibility, engagement, inquiries, and outcomes.

Do not optimize one area while neglecting the others.

A website that ranks but fails to communicate value is incomplete.

A website that looks professional but cannot be discovered is also incomplete.

## Search Intent

Search intent describes what a person is attempting to accomplish through a search.

Content should address the customer's actual objective rather than merely repeat search terms.

### Informational intent

The customer wants to understand a subject.

Example:

How often should a car be professionally detailed?

### Commercial investigation

The customer is evaluating available options.

Example:

Interior detailing versus full detailing.

### Transactional intent

The customer is prepared to take action.

Example:

Book an auto detail in Chattanooga.

### Navigational intent

The customer is searching for a specific business or website.

Example:

Mayes Auto Detailing.

### Local intent

The customer wants a relevant business within a geographic area.

Example:

Auto detailing near me.

Search intent categories may overlap.

Evaluate the actual search results and customer needs before assigning a page strategy.

## Search Intent Mapping

Assign meaningful search intent to appropriate pages.

| Page type | Primary purpose |
|---|---|
| Homepage | Business identity and primary offering |
| Services overview | Discover and compare services |
| Individual service page | Evaluate a specific service |
| About page | Understand the business and its credibility |
| Project page | Evaluate demonstrated work |
| FAQ content | Resolve relevant customer questions |
| Contact page | Initiate communication |
| Service area page | Understand geographic service availability |
| Product page | Evaluate and purchase a product |
| Collection page | Discover related products |

### Requirements

- Give every important page a defined purpose.
- Avoid unnecessary overlap between pages.
- Address customer questions naturally.
- Maintain meaningful relationships between pages.
- Avoid creating pages solely to target keyword variations.

Do not assign multiple near-identical pages to the same search intent without a legitimate content distinction.

## Keyword Research

Keyword research helps identify how potential customers describe their needs.

It informs content strategy but does not replace business knowledge or customer understanding.

### Research categories

Evaluate:

- Primary service terms.
- Secondary service terms.
- Geographic modifiers.
- Customer questions.
- Service comparison terms.
- Problem-based searches.
- Brand-related searches.
- Relevant long-tail searches.

### Illustrative example

For an auto-detailing business:

- Auto detailing.
- Interior car detailing.
- Exterior car polishing.
- Mobile auto detailing.
- Car detailing Chattanooga.
- How often should a car be detailed?
- Interior detailing versus full detailing.

These examples are illustrative, not verified search-volume findings.

### Evaluation criteria

Consider:

- Business relevance.
- Customer intent.
- Geographic relevance.
- Service availability.
- Competitive environment.
- Available supporting content.
- Conversion potential.

Do not prioritize a keyword solely because it has high estimated search volume.

## Website Information Architecture

Organic visibility begins with a meaningful website structure.

### Typical local-service architecture

```text
Home
Services
    Individual Service Pages
Our Work
    Individual Project Pages
About
Contact
Privacy Policy
```

Additional pages may be appropriate when justified by customer needs and business operations.

### Requirements

- Important services have appropriate representation.
- Navigation supports discovery.
- Page relationships are understandable.
- Internal links support related content.
- Important pages are not unnecessarily isolated.
- Duplicate content is avoided.
- Conversion destinations remain accessible.

Coordinate sitemap planning with Experience Design and Relume.

Do not create an unnecessarily large website merely to increase indexed page count.

## Search Algorithm and Platform Changes

Search platforms and visibility tools evolve.

### Evaluation procedure

When a consequential change occurs:

1. Identify the change.
2. Verify authoritative documentation.
3. Determine whether it affects Mayes Digital.
4. Identify affected websites or processes.
5. Distinguish confirmed impact from speculation.
6. Recommend appropriate action.
7. Update internal guidance when necessary.

### Requirements

- Avoid reacting to every industry rumor.
- Avoid unnecessary website changes after algorithm updates.
- Evaluate actual performance evidence.
- Preserve useful customer content.
- Avoid speculative ranking manipulation.

Focus on durable practices rather than short-term search tactics.

## AI Search and Emerging Discovery

Customers may discover businesses through AI-assisted search experiences and emerging platforms.

### Appropriate preparation

- Maintain accurate business information.
- Publish clear service descriptions.
- Provide authentic evidence.
- Maintain accessible website content.
- Use appropriate structured data.
- Preserve meaningful internal relationships.

### Requirements

- Verify current platform capabilities.
- Avoid unsupported claims about AI recommendation algorithms.
- Avoid inventing special ranking factors.
- Avoid guaranteeing inclusion in AI-generated answers.
- Evaluate emerging tools according to actual business value.

Do not introduce a separate speculative optimization framework when established content quality and technical discoverability practices already address the requirement.

- Doorway pages.
