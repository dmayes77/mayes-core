# Local Seo

Read for geographic relevance, local profiles, business information consistency, citations, authentic reviews, project evidence, and DetailFlow local discovery.

## Geographic Relevance

Local businesses should communicate where they operate and whom they serve.

### Geographic information

Depending on the business:

- Physical location.
- Primary service area.
- Surrounding communities.
- Mobile service availability.
- Travel limitations.
- Relevant geographic restrictions.

### Requirements

- Use accurate geographic information.
- Explain actual service availability.
- Include locations naturally.
- Maintain consistency across relevant platforms.
- Avoid implying a physical location that does not exist.

### Service area pages

Create dedicated service area pages only when they provide meaningful, distinct customer value.

Useful distinctions may include:

- Actual local project examples.
- Different service availability.
- Location-specific operational information.
- Relevant local customer questions.

### Prohibited practices

- Mass-producing near-identical city pages.
- Creating fake business addresses.
- Claiming nonexistent locations.
- Adding unrelated cities solely for keyword coverage.
- Creating doorway pages.

A legitimate service area page must provide more than a substituted city name.

## Local SEO

Local SEO helps relevant customers discover businesses within their geographic area.

### Core areas

- Google Business Profile.
- Accurate business information.
- Appropriate business categories.
- Website relevance.
- Authentic customer reviews.
- Legitimate local references.
- Geographic service clarity.

### Google local ranking considerations

Google describes local results primarily through:

- Relevance.
- Distance.
- Prominence.

Verify current official guidance before making specific recommendations.

### Requirements

- Improve business relevance through accurate information.
- Strengthen legitimate credibility.
- Maintain appropriate geographic representation.
- Avoid unsupported ranking guarantees.

Distance is not a factor that can be legitimately manipulated through fabricated locations.

## Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile is an important discovery resource for eligible local businesses.

### Profile evaluation

Review:

- Business name.
- Primary category.
- Additional categories.
- Address or service area.
- Telephone number.
- Website URL.
- Business hours.
- Holiday hours.
- Business description.
- Services or products.
- Business photographs.
- Customer reviews.
- Relevant profile attributes.

### Requirements

- Use the real-world business name.
- Select accurate categories.
- Maintain correct business information.
- Follow applicable eligibility requirements.
- Use authentic business photographs.
- Maintain appropriate profile access.

### Prohibited practices

- Keyword stuffing the business name.
- Creating fake locations.
- Creating duplicate profiles without legitimate justification.
- Misrepresenting service areas.
- Fabricating reviews.
- Making unauthorized profile changes.

### Ownership

The business should retain appropriate ownership and access to its profile.

Mayes Digital should receive only the permissions necessary to provide authorized services.

## Business Information Consistency

Maintain accurate and consistent business information across relevant digital properties.

Commonly referred to as NAP:

- Name.
- Address.
- Phone.

### Additional information

Also evaluate:

- Website URL.
- Business hours.
- Service areas.
- Business categories.
- Relevant contact information.

### Core implementation

Use the established Business Settings and Business Hours architecture where applicable.

Avoid independently hardcoding business information throughout the website.

### Consistency requirements

The website, Google Business Profile, relevant directories, and structured data should communicate compatible business information.

Minor formatting differences do not automatically constitute significant visibility problems.

Prioritize material inaccuracies.

## Authentic Review Strategy

Customer reviews can help potential customers evaluate a business.

### Appropriate practices

- Request reviews from actual customers.
- Make the review process understandable.
- Provide accurate review links.
- Encourage honest feedback.
- Respond professionally.
- Respect applicable platform policies.

### Review presentation

Use authentic reviews on the website where appropriate.

Preserve:

- Original meaning.
- Accurate ratings.
- Appropriate attribution.
- Applicable permissions.

### Prohibited practices

- Purchasing reviews.
- Fabricating reviews.
- Creating fictional customer identities.
- Selectively soliciting only positive reviews in violation of platform policies.
- Offering prohibited incentives.
- Misrepresenting aggregate ratings.

When reviews are unavailable, strengthen other legitimate credibility resources.

## Project-Based Credibility

Authentic completed work is a valuable organic visibility asset.

### Appropriate content

- Completed project photographs.
- Before-and-after comparisons.
- Project descriptions.
- Service details.
- Relevant geographic context.
- Customer challenges.
- Actual project outcomes.

### Project page requirements

Create dedicated project pages when sufficient useful information is available.

Each project page should provide meaningful context rather than merely repeat a generic description.

### Core implementation

Evaluate:

- Projects CMS.
- Project cards.
- Project collection pages.
- Before-and-after components.
- Existing image architecture.

### Requirements

- Use authentic project evidence.
- Obtain appropriate permissions.
- Avoid exposing private customer information.
- Do not represent stock photography as completed work.
- Do not fabricate outcomes.
