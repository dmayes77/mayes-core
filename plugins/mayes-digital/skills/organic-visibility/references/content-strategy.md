# Content Strategy

Read for page and metadata planning, content quality, links, lead journeys, ownership, and product-specific content.

## Homepage Optimization

The homepage establishes the business's identity, primary offering, and relevance.

### Required considerations

- Clear business identity.
- Understandable primary offering.
- Relevant geographic context.
- Meaningful service presentation.
- Authentic credibility.
- Accessible contact information.
- Appropriate conversion actions.
- Logical internal links.

### Heading example

Professional Auto Detailing in Chattanooga

This is an illustrative heading.

Adapt wording to the actual business, service area, and customer needs.

### Avoid

- Vague marketing slogans as the only primary heading.
- Excessive geographic keyword repetition.
- Unsupported claims.
- Generic service descriptions.
- Unnecessary keyword lists.
- Hidden search-oriented text.

The homepage should communicate clearly to customers before attempting to satisfy search engines.

## Service Overview Pages

A service overview page helps customers discover and compare available services.

### Requirements

- Present actual business services.
- Use understandable service names.
- Explain meaningful differences.
- Provide appropriate service links.
- Include relevant pricing when available.
- Maintain consistent presentation.
- Support appropriate conversion actions.

### Core implementation

Evaluate existing:

- Section / Service List.
- Section / Card Grid.
- Card / Service.
- Relevant CMS relationships.
- Existing price visibility properties.

Verify current component availability.

### Avoid

- Creating artificial service categories.
- Listing services the business does not provide.
- Repeating identical descriptions.
- Adding unnecessary keyword variations as separate services.

## Individual Service Pages

Create individual service pages when the service has sufficient customer relevance and distinct content.

### Recommended content

- Service-specific heading.
- Clear service description.
- Scope of service.
- Customer benefits.
- Relevant limitations.
- Pricing or quote information.
- Authentic project evidence.
- Service-specific questions.
- Appropriate conversion action.

### Illustrative architecture

```text
Service Hero
Service Overview
Scope of Service
Benefits
Relevant Project Evidence
Process
Frequently Asked Questions
Final CTA
```

Adapt composition according to available content and customer needs.

### Requirements

- Address the service's actual search intent.
- Use accurate business terminology.
- Provide distinct and useful information.
- Link to relevant supporting pages.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Maintain clear conversion paths.

Do not create individual pages for trivial keyword variations.

## Content Quality

Content should help customers understand the business and make informed decisions.

### Content standards

Content should be:

- Accurate.
- Relevant.
- Understandable.
- Useful.
- Original where appropriate.
- Consistent with actual operations.
- Written for the intended audience.

### Business experience

Where available, incorporate:

- Firsthand service experience.
- Practical explanations.
- Authentic project examples.
- Actual customer questions.
- Verified qualifications.
- Real operational processes.

### E-E-A-T considerations

Google's quality evaluation guidance discusses:

- Experience.
- Expertise.
- Authoritativeness.
- Trustworthiness.

Use these concepts to evaluate content credibility.

Do not represent E-E-A-T as a directly measurable ranking score or a single ranking factor.

## Content Strategy

Develop content around meaningful customer needs.

### Priority order

1. Accurate foundational business pages.
2. Complete service information.
3. Authentic credibility resources.
4. Relevant customer questions.
5. Useful supporting educational content.

### Supporting content examples

- Service preparation guides.
- Maintenance recommendations.
- Service comparisons.
- Common customer concerns.
- Seasonal considerations.
- Authentic project explanations.

### Blog requirements

A blog is optional.

Do not recommend a blog solely because conventional SEO advice suggests that every website needs one.

Recommend ongoing publishing only when the business has:

- Relevant topics.
- Useful expertise.
- Sufficient content quality.
- A sustainable maintenance process.

Publishing frequency is not a substitute for content usefulness.

## AI-Assisted Content

AI may support content planning, drafting, organization, and editing.

### Appropriate uses

- Developing content outlines.
- Organizing verified business information.
- Improving readability.
- Identifying customer questions.
- Drafting metadata.
- Creating initial service descriptions.
- Reviewing content consistency.

### Requirements

- Verify factual claims.
- Preserve the business's actual voice.
- Review service descriptions.
- Confirm geographic information.
- Confirm pricing and operational claims.
- Remove unsupported statements.
- Obtain appropriate business approval.

### Prohibited practices

- Mass-producing low-value pages.
- Inventing firsthand experience.
- Fabricating business history.
- Generating fictional testimonials.
- Creating unsupported qualifications.
- Publishing misleading information.

AI-assisted content must provide genuine customer value.

## Page Title Standards

Page titles should accurately communicate page relevance.

### Requirements

- Describe the page's actual content.
- Use meaningful service terminology.
- Include geographic context when appropriate.
- Distinguish important pages.
- Include business branding where useful.
- Avoid excessive repetition.

### Illustrative patterns

Homepage:

```text
[Primary Service] in [Location] | [Business Name]
```

Service page:

```text
[Specific Service] in [Location] | [Business Name]
```

Project page:

```text
[Project Description] | [Business Name]
```

These are starting patterns, not mandatory formulas.

### Length considerations

Write concise, descriptive titles.

Do not treat a fixed character count as an absolute search engine requirement.

Search engines may generate or rewrite displayed title links.

## Meta Description Standards

Meta descriptions should summarize the page accurately and help relevant customers understand its value.

### Requirements

- Describe actual page content.
- Communicate meaningful relevance.
- Use natural language.
- Include appropriate geographic context.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Distinguish important pages.

### Illustrative example

Professional interior and exterior auto detailing in Chattanooga. Explore available services and request a quote for your vehicle.

### Important distinction

Meta descriptions are not guaranteed to appear exactly as written in search results.

Do not claim that meta descriptions directly improve rankings.

Evaluate them as useful search-result communication resources.

## URL Architecture

Use understandable, maintainable URLs.

### Illustrative examples

```text
/services
/services/interior-detailing
/services/exterior-polishing
/our-work
/contact
```

### Requirements

- Use meaningful slugs.
- Preserve logical page relationships.
- Avoid unnecessary parameters.
- Avoid excessive keyword repetition.
- Maintain stable URLs where practical.
- Establish redirects when important URLs change.

### URL changes

Before changing an existing published URL:

- Evaluate current visibility.
- Identify existing internal links.
- Identify relevant external references.
- Establish appropriate redirects.
- Update canonical references where necessary.
- Verify the resulting destination.

Do not change established URLs solely for minor stylistic preferences.

## Internal Linking

Internal links help customers and search engines discover related content.

### Appropriate relationships

- Homepage to services.
- Services overview to individual services.
- Service pages to relevant projects.
- Project pages to associated services.
- Relevant educational content to service pages.
- Service pages to appropriate conversion destinations.

### Requirements

- Use meaningful link text.
- Link to relevant destinations.
- Avoid broken links.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Preserve customer navigation clarity.
- Avoid excessive keyword-oriented links.

### Example

Prefer:

Explore our interior detailing service.

Rather than:

Click here.

Use descriptive links when they improve understanding.
