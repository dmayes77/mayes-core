# Mayes Digital Organic Visibility

Build discoverability around real customer intent, accurate business information, and owned credibility. Favor relevant inquiries over traffic without business value. Do not invent reviews, qualifications, locations, pricing, or ranking claims.

## Scope and evidence

Establish the business, services, locations, customers, existing website and profile state, and the requested outcome. Distinguish observed facts, platform requirements, opportunities, and hypotheses. Verify current search guidance against official Google, Bing, Schema.org, and Webflow documentation when it affects a decision. Consult the current `dmayes77/mayes-core` architecture for implementation constraints.

Mayes Digital's standard service scope is organic discovery: search, local profiles, authentic reviews, content, relevant directories, referrals, and measurement. Paid campaign management is outside that standard scope. Do not claim that paid advertising is ineffective.

Evaluate the chain from foundation and discoverability through relevance, credibility, conversion, and measurement. A recommendation should identify the customer intent, supporting evidence, proposed change, implementation owner, and a way to measure the outcome. Avoid guarantees of rankings or traffic.

## Ownership

Organic Visibility defines search intent, page and content strategy, local profile needs, metadata requirements, and discoverability opportunities. Experience Design owns customer journeys and usability. Core Design System owns structural and visual standards. Webflow Development implements approved technical changes. Quality Assurance verifies the result. Surface conflicts with approved architecture or observed platform behavior.

- [Search strategy](references/search-strategy.md): research, intent mapping, information architecture, platform changes, and ethical search decisions.
- [Local SEO](references/local-seo.md): geographic relevance, business profiles, consistent business data, citations, reviews, and project credibility.
- [Content strategy](references/content-strategy.md): service pages, content quality, metadata planning, links, organic lead journeys, and product content.
- [Technical SEO](references/technical-seo.md): crawlability, sitemaps, canonicals, redirects, structured data, images, mobile behavior, and performance requirements.
- [Measurement](references/measurement.md): Search Console, profile and analytics evidence, reporting, audits, findings, and prioritization.

Read references when their topic is in scope. For a local-business website audit, combine the relevant references; for a page title edit, use the content reference and current platform guidance.

## Finish with a measurable recommendation

State the observed condition and evidence, the customer or business impact, the recommended action, who owns implementation, and what remains unverified. Keep platform access, tracking limits, and expected outcomes explicit. Do not claim an improvement occurred until it was implemented and measured.
