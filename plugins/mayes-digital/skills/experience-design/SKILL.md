# Mayes Digital Experience Design

Make the customer's goal clear and practical to complete. Every recommendation should serve an identifiable customer need or business objective, with accessibility, usability, clarity, trust, and conversion guiding the decision. Use UX principles as decision aids, not universal rules or predictions of behavior.

## Understand the experience

Identify the audience, business goal, key customer task, current journey, available content, and constraints. Inspect direct entry points as well as the homepage. For a local business, consider discovery, evaluation, confidence, action, and confirmation; customers may enter at any stage.

For Mayes Digital implementation constraints, consult the current `dmayes77/mayes-core` architecture and decisions. Check actual Webflow component availability before recommending reuse. Relume may accelerate sitemaps and wireframes, but approved Core standards govern implementation.

When principles compete, weigh accessibility, the user's immediate objective, the importance and visibility of information, task complexity, user expectations, available evidence, and maintenance cost. Do not hide essential information simply to reduce visual complexity.

## Keep responsibilities clear

Experience Design defines the journey, hierarchy, interactions, and reasons behind proposed choices. Core Design System defines variables, classes, components, and responsive structure. Organic Visibility defines search intent and discoverability requirements. Webflow Development implements approved behavior. Quality Assurance independently verifies accessibility, function, performance, and readiness.

## Open only the relevant reference

- [UX principles](references/ux-principles.md): select and balance principles, explain design reasoning, and distinguish evidence from hypotheses.
- [Webflow examples](references/webflow-examples.md): apply experience decisions to Relume plans, pages, navigation, forms, mobile layouts, and existing Core resources.
- [Audit checklist](references/audit-checklist.md): assess existing pages, components, journeys, wireframes, findings, priorities, and validation.

Use only the references relevant to the task. A mobile journey audit typically needs the audit checklist and selected Webflow examples; a principle question needs the UX principles reference.

## Recommend and verify

Describe the observed experience, customer objective, evidence, relevant principle, proposed change, likely benefit, Core implementation path, and validation method. Separate confirmed usability issues from preferences and hypotheses. Prioritize demonstrated impact over the number of findings. Do not fabricate customer evidence or improvement percentages, use deceptive conversion patterns, or claim testing that did not occur.
